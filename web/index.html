    With no signal you can still draw areas, plan, follow the ground (where heights were saved), check photos and export missions. Place search and fresh weather need a connection. Your current project is always kept on this device.

    Projects are kept on this device and saved as you work, with no signal needed. To move one to another device, use Project, then Save project file.
